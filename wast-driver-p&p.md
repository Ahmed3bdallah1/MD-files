# Privacy Policy — Wasta Driver

**Effective date:** 24 November 2026
**App:** Wasta Driver (Android) — the courier/driver app of the Wasta delivery platform
**Operator:** Wasta ("we", "us", "our")
**Contact:** [qeemamobile@gmail.com] — replace with your real address before publishing

This policy explains what information the Wasta Driver app collects, why, how it is shared, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

## 1. Information we collect

**Account and profile information** — name, phone number, email address, profile photo, and any identity or vehicle details you submit during registration and verification.

**Location data** — your precise and approximate location (GPS/network). It is collected while the app is open and you are online/available for orders, so we can match you with nearby orders, show your position to the customer and the merchant during a delivery, and calculate routes and distances. The app does not collect location when you are offline or have closed it.

**Photos** — images you choose from your gallery or take with your camera (for example, a profile picture or documents). The app only accesses images you select.

**Orders, shifts and earnings** — orders you accept and complete, delivery status, shifts you book and start, break requests, working hours, wallet balance, earnings reports, and withdrawal requests.

**Device and technical data** — device model, operating-system version, app version, language, push-notification token, and diagnostic data such as crash information.

**Communications** — messages and support requests you send to us, and push notifications we send to you (new orders, shift reminders, wallet updates).

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; the app is for adult drivers only.

## 2. How we use your information

- Create and verify your driver account and keep it secure
- Assign orders, show live delivery tracking, and navigate you to pickup and drop-off points
- Manage shifts, breaks, wallet balance, earnings, and withdrawals
- Send order, shift and account notifications
- Provide customer support and resolve disputes or incidents
- Detect fraud, abuse and misuse of the platform
- Maintain, debug and improve the app
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

## 3. Permissions the app requests

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Location (precise and approximate) | Order matching, live tracking, routing |
| Notifications | Alerts for new orders, shifts and wallet activity |
| Camera / photos | Profile photo and documents you choose to upload |
| Internet access | Communicate with our servers |
| Vibrate, wake lock, start at boot | Alert you to incoming orders and keep notifications reliable |

You can change or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings. Turning off location will stop you from receiving and delivering orders.

## 4. Who we share information with

- **Customers and merchants** — while you deliver an order they can see your first name, photo, vehicle details, phone contact (as needed to complete the order) and live location.
- **Service providers** that process data on our behalf: **Google Firebase** (push notifications, app infrastructure), **Google Maps** (maps and routing), and our hosting and real-time messaging infrastructure. These providers are bound to use data only to provide their services.
- **Authorities or other parties** when required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights, safety and property of users, us or the public.
- **Business transfers** — if we merge with or are acquired by another company, your data may be transferred, subject to this policy.

## 5. Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as needed for the purposes above. After you delete your account we remove or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes (such as completed order and payout records).

## 6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and access to it is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your login details private and report any suspected unauthorised access to us immediately.

## 7. Your rights and choices

You may access, correct or update your profile data in the app, withdraw permissions in device settings, opt out of non-essential notifications, and request a copy or deletion of your data.

**Account and data deletion:** email [privacy@your-domain.com] from your registered address (or use the delete-account option in the app if available) with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm and process the request within 30 days.

Where applicable, you also have rights under Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020, including the right to know, access, correct, and object to processing of your data.

## 8. International transfers

Our service providers (such as Google) may process data in countries outside Egypt. Where this happens we rely on appropriate safeguards offered by those providers.

## 9. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 18.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at the link on our Google Play listing, and we will notify you in the app for material changes.
