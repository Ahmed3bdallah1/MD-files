# Privacy Policy

**App:** My Order Client
**Last updated:** 2026-11-08

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **[QeemaTech]** ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the My Order Client mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide
- **Account information:** name, phone number, email address, and password when you register or sign in.
- **Verification data:** one-time codes (OTP/PIN) used to verify your account or reset your password.
- **Order information:** items ordered, delivery addresses, notes, and order history.
- **Files and images:** photos or documents you choose to upload (for example, a profile picture or an attachment) through the camera, gallery, or file picker.
- **Communications:** messages or feedback you send to us or to support.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically
- **Location data:** your precise or approximate location (GPS and network), used to set a delivery address, show nearby services, and display order tracking on a map. Location is collected only after you grant permission.
- **Device and usage information:** device model, operating system version, app version, language, IP address, and crash and diagnostic data.
- **Push notification token:** a unique identifier issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send you notifications.
- **Connectivity status:** whether your device is online, to handle network errors gracefully.

### 2.3 Information stored on your device
- **Secure storage:** authentication tokens and other sensitive values are kept in the platform's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
- **Local storage:** preferences (such as language) and cached data are kept locally to improve performance and offline experience.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information to:
- Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
- Process, deliver, and track your orders in real time.
- Show maps, routes, and delivery progress.
- Send order updates, security alerts, and (with your permission) promotional notifications.
- Provide customer support and respond to your requests.
- Maintain, secure, and improve the App, including fixing bugs and delivering updates.
- Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Device Permissions

| Permission | Why we need it | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Internet / network state | Communicate with our servers and detect connectivity | Yes |
| Location (fine / coarse) | Delivery address, nearby services, order tracking | Optional |
| Notifications | Order status updates and alerts | Optional |
| Camera / photos / files | Uploading images or documents you select | Optional |

You can change or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings. Some features may not work without them.

## 5. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third parties to operate the App. They process data only as needed to provide their services and under their own privacy policies:

- **Firebase (Google):** push notifications (Cloud Messaging), remote configuration, and core services. <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- **Google Maps Platform:** map display, geocoding, and route drawing. <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- **Real-time messaging (WebSocket / Reverb):** live order status updates.
- **Shorebird:** delivery of over-the-air app updates.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 6. How We Share Information

We may share information:
- **With service providers** who help us run the App (hosting, notifications, maps, analytics).
- **With merchants and delivery personnel** to the extent needed to fulfil your order (for example, your name, phone number, and delivery address).
- **For legal reasons,** if required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and security.
- **In a business transfer,** such as a merger or acquisition, with notice to you where required.

## 7. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

## 8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure on-device storage for credentials. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
- Delete your account and associated data.
- Withdraw consent (for example, disable location or notifications in device settings).
- Object to or restrict certain processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us at **[support@example.com]**. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

## 10. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting **[support@example.com]**. Some data may be retained where required by law (for example, order and transaction records).

## 11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under **13** (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed on servers located outside your country. Where required, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.
