## 1. Introduction

Wasta ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the Wasta mobile app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using the app you agree to this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

**Account information**
- Name, email address, phone number, and password (stored hashed) when you register or sign in.
- Profile details you choose to add, such as a profile picture.

**Location information**
- Your device location, when you grant permission, to show nearby services and providers, and to set delivery or service addresses.
- Addresses you save in the app.

**Photos and files**
- Images you select or take with the camera or gallery (for example, a profile photo or images attached to a conversation).

**Microphone / voice**
- Audio is processed for voice search when you tap the voice-search button. Speech recognition is performed by your device's speech service (which may be provided by Google); we do not store the recordings.

**Messages and activity**
- Messages you send and receive in conversations, ratings and reviews you submit, and your cart, orders, and coupon usage.

**Device and technical data**
- Device identifiers and push-notification tokens, app version, operating system, and diagnostic information needed to deliver notifications and keep the app working.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
- To show nearby results, process orders and requests, and enable chat.
- To send notifications about orders, messages, and updates.
- To improve, secure, and troubleshoot the app.
- To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may process your data under their own privacy policies:

| Service | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) | Push notifications |
| Google Maps / Google Places (Google) | Maps, location search, address lookup |
| Device speech recognition | Voice search |

## 5. Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| Location | Nearby results and address selection |
| Microphone | Voice search |
| Camera / Photos | Attaching or uploading images |
| Notifications | Order and message alerts |
| Internet | Communicating with our servers |

You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings. Some features will not work if you deny them.

## 6. Data Sharing

We share information only:
- With service providers who help us operate the app (hosting, notifications, maps).
- With other users or providers where needed to fulfil your request (for example, your name and address for an order, or messages in a conversation).
- When required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.

## 7. Data Retention

We keep your information while your account is active and as needed to provide the service or meet legal obligations. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data, except where we must retain it by law.

## 8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## 9. Your Rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by contacting us. You can also update your profile in the app or delete your account [in-app / by request].

## 10. Children's Privacy

Wasta is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change, and we will notify you of significant changes in the app.
